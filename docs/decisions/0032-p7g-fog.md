# ADR-0032: P7g fog parity

Status: accepted (2026-10-31)

## Decision

One pure model (`umicraft-render/src/fog.rs`) decides the fog colour and the
falloff of every pass. Its inputs are `RenderEnvironment` plus a `FogContext`
(medium at the eye, effects, eye light) that the game thread fills. The shaders
share `fog.wgsl`: a density above zero selects exponential fog, otherwise the
linear `start..end` band.

## Consequences

- Terrain, entities, clouds and the sky dome fade by the same colour and mode;
  the sky dome keeps its own `setupFog(-1)` range.
- The terrain fog is linear (the reference `GL_LINEAR`) instead of the
  ADR-0008 smoothstep. The terrain fog target is still darkened by the vertex
  light (ADR-0008 §3), a deliberate deviation.
- Exponential fog uses the radial distance (the shaders have it); OpenGL uses
  the depth. Respiration, Aqua Affinity, cloud fog and rain in the eye light
  are not modelled.
- The eye light is eased on the render thread, once per received tick, like
  `fogColor1`.
